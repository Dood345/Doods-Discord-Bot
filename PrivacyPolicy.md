Privacy Policy for Cave Johnson

This Privacy Policy governs the collection, use, and sharing of personal information by Cave Johnson, a Discord bot developed by Dood345. By using Cave Johnson, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide to us through your use of the bot, such as your Discord user ID and username, server and channel information, and message content. We may also collect usage data, such as the frequency and duration of your use of the bot.

Specifically:
- AI chat memory: when you mention the bot or use its AI commands, your message and the bot's reply are stored with your Discord user ID. This memory is shared across every server the bot is in, so the bot remembers you everywhere. If you reply to another person's message while mentioning the bot, that message's author name and text are included in the AI request.
- Roasts and compliments: when someone uses the roast or compliment commands on you, your display name and your recent AI chat messages with the bot are used to personalize the response.
- Conversation summary: older AI chat messages are condensed into a short private summary linked to your user ID so the bot keeps context over time. /memory shows it and /clearhistory deletes it.
- Conversation search index: older exchanges are also turned into numeric search vectors (computed on the bot owner's own hardware, never sent to outside services) so the bot can recall a past conversation when it becomes relevant again. Recalled excerpts come only from your own chats. /clearhistory deletes the index along with your chat memory.
- Science Points: in each server, the bot records point awards (for example, one point for the first message you send there each day, and Test Chamber trivia results, including which answer you picked) and achievements, linked to your user ID and that server. Message content is not stored for this. /points and /leaderboard show them. /clearhistory does not reset points; ask the bot owner to remove them.
- Birthdays: only if you run /birthday set in a server, the bot stores your birth month and day (no year) for that server and posts a shout-out on the day. /birthday remove deletes it.
- Remembered notes: if you ask the bot to remember something about you (or tell it a preference), it may save a short note (up to 20) linked to your user ID and use it in future chats. Use /memory to see your notes; /clearhistory deletes them along with your chat memory.
- Server data: game library entries, ratings, tags and gift wishlists you add are stored with your user ID and the server ID.
- Logs: operational logs (which may include usernames and command usage) are kept in rotating files limited to about 30 MB in total, after which the oldest entries are overwritten.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to operate and improve Cave Johnson, including to provide support and respond to your requests. We may also use your information to develop new features or services, to conduct research and analytics, and to comply with legal obligations.

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties. AI responses are generated by a language model hosted on the bot owner's own hardware; message content is not sent to a third-party AI provider. Music requests are looked up on YouTube, which sees the search terms but not your Discord identity. However, we may disclose your information in response to legal process or a request from a law enforcement agency or regulatory authority.

Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide Cave Johnson’s services or as required by law. AI chat memory is kept until you delete it. You can erase your own AI chat memory at any time with the /clearhistory command. For any other deletion request, contact us using the details below.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction. However, no security measure is perfect, and we cannot guarantee the security of your information.
